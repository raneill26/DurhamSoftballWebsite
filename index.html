Our league has partnered with local non-profit organizations and businesses to create a fun, competitive, and rewarding softball league. All proceeds from the league are donated to our partner charities and teams will compete during the season to earn a larger share of the donation pool for their charity. The more the team wins, the more their charity will receive!

About the league

Durham Softball is a charity co-ed adult softball league in Durham, NC. The league is managed by Play NC, a 501(c)3 non-profit that hosts sports and games for charity.

$70,000+

Donated to Durham non-profits since 2018, plus thousands of needed items.

Win = give

The more you win and score, the more donated to the charity on your jersey.

Year-round

There are two seasons in a year that span from March-June and August-October.
