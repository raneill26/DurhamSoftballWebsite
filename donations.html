Our main goal is to host a great game, make a profit on registrations, and give that profit away to worthy local causes. Any extra or unexpected earnings just add to our Donation Pool and help us give more support to organizations providing real help to people in our community.

Where the money goes

Here is a rough breakdown of expenses, which vary slightly every year:

  • 60% of all entry fees collected are spent locally to provide a fun and rewarding softball experience: field reservations, umpires, staff, jerseys, prizes.
  • 25% of all registrations go directly to our local non-profit partners, earned by teams during the season and donated at the end of the year.
  • 15% of all entry fees go to outside vendors: supply orders, credit card fees, website fees.

What it costs to run the league

Softball has some very predictable expenses. Here is a breakdown of exactly what it takes to run this game and where registration fees are spent.

  • Field use through Durham Parks & Recreation is $20 per hour. With 8 games a week for 12 weeks including playoffs, that is $1,920 in field reservations.
  • Umpires earn $30 per game. Across 96 regular season games and 13 playoff games, $3,660.
  • The field attendant earns $15 per hour. Twelve weeks at 6.5 hours a week, $1,170.
  • A brand new softball every game, about $5 each, or $545.
  • Insurance runs about $140 per team per season, roughly $2,240.
  • Every player gets a custom jersey at about $7 each, roughly $1,792.
  • Bases, scorecards, print materials, website and other small items, about $500 a season.
  • PayPal fees take about 2.75% of all payments, an estimated $492.

Total: about $12,319 in estimated operating expenses.

What your registration also does

Along with paying the normal costs of organising a league, your registration will:

  • Sponsor an estimated $4,000 per season in direct cash donations to our charity sponsors.
  • Support Play NC's Miracle League of the Triangle team, the Junior League White Sox (about $1,500 a year).
  • Sponsor a Cat Cottage at the Animal Protection Society of Durham ($300 a year).
  • Sponsor a baseball and a softball team for South Durham Little League ($1,200 a year).
  • Support the off-season donation fund, which helps local causes and individuals with needs the league cannot anticipate in advance.

After expenses and guaranteed donations, there is usually between $3,000 and $4,000 left over each season to fund the Donation Pools. That means roughly 20% of your registration fee is donated to our partner non-profits.

Three promises

As a non-profit league focused on helping Durham, every possible dollar goes toward one of three things:

  1. Providing a great softball experience, or
  2. Patronising a Durham business, or
  3. Helping a Durham non-profit.

Umpires and field attendants are the only people earning personal money from Play NC events. Ryan works for free. More than 75% of all funds received are re-invested in Durham individuals or organisations by the end of the season.

We appreciate your support.
