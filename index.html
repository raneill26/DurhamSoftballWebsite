Why the league exists

The softball is how we fund the work.

Durham Softball is a charity co-ed adult softball league in Durham, NC. The league is managed by Play NC, a 501(c)3 non-profit that hosts sports and games for charity.

$70,000+

Donated to Durham non-profits since 2018, plus thousands of needed items.

Win = give

The more you win and score, the more donated to the charity on your jersey.

Year-round

There are two seasons in a year that span from March to June and August-October.
