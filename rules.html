League Info

Rules

Co-ed, recreational, adult, slow-pitch. Play to have fun, make friends, get exercise, and help local charities.

The Durham Softball League is a co-ed, recreational, adult, slow-pitch softball league. Players must temper their competitive spirit with good sportsmanship and always attempt to avoid injury, both for themselves and others. Individuals who cannot translate these goals into attitudes and actions on the field should not play in this league.

Safety comes first

Participants must recognize that, as in any sport, there is potential for significant injury. Some of your teammates and opponents may have little or no experience playing softball, which makes injuries more likely. For their own safety, all players should learn proper techniques of batting, fielding, and base running and instruct less skilled players in those techniques.

All players are responsible for playing the game as safely as possible.

It is the responsibility of team captains and umpires to monitor player conduct and take appropriate action to correct objectionable behavior. Teammates of offending players should exert pressure to stop offensive behavior peer pressure is our most effective means of enforcement. Umpires are charged with ejecting players if, in their opinion, the situation warrants it.

Permission to use the playing fields is contingent on our respect for the facility and our behavior. You are expected to remove all trash from the field when you leave.

Code of conduct

  • Abusive language, profanity, and vulgar behavior will not be tolerated and can result in players and/or coaches being suspended.
  • Do not insult opposing players or umpires.
  • Park only in designated parking lots this includes motorcycles and vans. Do not park on the grass.
  • No loud music. Audio equipment is not allowed on the field.
  • Fighting is an automatic suspension for one calendar year.
  • No smoking, and no unused equipment in the playing area.
  • No alcoholic beverages or grills at the field.

Basically: if you can't play nice, you'll be asked to leave. Please play nice.

Rainouts, cancellations and scheduled games

We use Durham Parks & Recreation facilities and have a hard deadline to finish the season before all DPR facilities close, July 1 for Summer and November 1 for Winter. Every effort to play all our games each week will be made, but DPR will ultimately decide if the fields are playable and determine if we can use them each day.

We want to play at least 8 or 9 regular season games each season, and we schedule as many regular season games as possible. If we never get rained out, there is no room to reschedule, because every possible week has already been scheduled. If we do get rained out, those games are cancelled entirely. This is to ensure the full season finishes before the fields close.

For every game cancelled we add $25 per team to the end-of-season Donation Pool. Rather than a per-game cancellation refund, a higher percentage of your registration fee goes to our partner non-profits. At the end of the season we total any amounts owed from cancellations and add that to your team's earned Donation Pool.

Choosing a sponsor and team balancing

There are a maximum of 16 roster spots on each Free Agent team and 20 roster spots on each Full Team registration. If one charity does not reach 16 player registrations, some players may be asked to change teams and play for another cause to balance the rosters. This ensures all sponsors can field a full team each week and reduces the chance a team forfeits, so balancing should make the league more fun for everyone. Our goal is for every team to have at least 13 players so we can ignore this entire section.

If you have successfully registered for one of the 16 roster spots for your preferred sponsor, team balancing will never mean you are forced to play for another non-profit. Every possible accommodation that can be made, will be made. We believe all of our sponsors are worthy of your time and donations, so we hope you would not mind playing for any of our organizations if it made the league better for everyone.

All teams are also required to have at least 5 female players, so male registrations may be limited for a team that does not already have 5 women on it. If we cannot place you on your preferred team and an alternate charity is not sufficient, a full refund will be granted.

If one or more teams fail to receive at least 12 player registrations, the league may contract that team and distribute its members to other organizations for the season. Players are asked to change teams in reverse order of their registration, so sign up early to secure your spot on the team of your choice.
