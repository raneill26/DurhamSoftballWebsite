About Durham Softball

A charity co-ed adult softball league in Durham, NC, managed by Play NC a 501(c)(3) that hosts sports and games for charity.

Durham Softball pairs local non-profits with teams of players who mostly just want a reason to be outside on a Sunday. The competition is real but the stakes are friendly and all proceeds go to our partner charities.

How it works

Sign up as an individual or bring a group. Teams are balanced by the league, each one adopts a Durham non-profit, and the season standings decide how the donation pool gets split. The more your team wins, the more your charity receives.

Just bring a glove.

Since 2018, Play NC has donated more than $70,000 and thousands of needed items to local charities in Durham.

Season rhythm

  • Spring: registration opens in January, season starts in March.
  • Summer: registration in spring, season runs August through October.
  • Game day: Sundays at Pineywood Park, 9:10 AM to roughly 6:20 PM.

What the league funds

Beyond the seasonal donation pool, Durham Softball carries three standing sponsorships.

Annual sponsor

South Durham Little League

We sponsor at least one youth team every year, providing a baseball experience for kids ages 4 to 16.

Est. annual cost: $600 per team

Annual sponsor

Miracle League of the Triangle

The original inspiration for this league. Miracle League provides a baseball experience to children with special needs in Durham.

Est. annual cost: $1,500 per year

Annual sponsor

Animal Protection Society of Durham

We sponsor a kennel helping feed, shelter, and provide medical care for nearly 4,000 animals each year.

Est. annual cost: $300 per year
