How the pool works

Every player's league fee goes into a single seasonal donation pool. At the end of the season the pool is divided among the partner non-profits, weighted by where each team finished in the standings. A team that wins its league sends a bigger check than a team that finishes last — but every partner gets a share.

That's the whole competitive hook: you're not playing for a trophy, you're playing for someone else's budget.

How are funds used?

Distributions go directly to the partner organizations, who use them where they need them most. Alongside the seasonal pool, Play NC carries three standing annual sponsorships:

  • South Durham Little League — at least one youth team sponsored each year (~$600/team).
  • Miracle League of the Triangle — baseball for children with special needs (~$1,500/year).
  • Animal Protection Society of Durham — a sponsored kennel (~$300/year).

In addition to cash, the league runs donation drives for physical goods — food, books, pet supplies, household items — matched to what partners actually need that season.

Play NC is a 501(c)(3) non-profit. Donations are tax-deductible. Questions about giving? playncinc@gmail.com
